णमोकार मंत्र

Stuti

हिन्दी

णमो अरिहंताणं
णमो सिद्धाणं
णमो आयरियाणं
णमो उवज्झायाणं
णमो लोए सव्व साहूणं

एसोपंचणमोक्कारो, सव्वपावप्पणासणो
मंगला णं च सव्वेसिं, पडमम हवई मंगलं

ગુજરાતી

ણમો અરિહંતાણં
ણમો સિદ્ધાણં
ણમો આયરિયાણં
ણમો ઉવજ્ઝાયાણં
ણમો લોએ સવ્વ સાહૂણં

એસોપંચણમોક્કારો, સવ્વપાવપ્પણાસણો
મંગલા ણં ચ સવ્વેસિં, પડમમ હવઈ મંગલં

English

Namo Arihantanam
Namo Siddhanam
Namo Ayariyanam
Namo Uvajjhayanam
Namo Loye Savva Sahunam

Eso Panch Namokaro
Savva Pavappanasano
Mangalanam Cha Savvesim
Padhamam Havai Mangalam

Meaning

Namo Arihantanam: I bow in reverence to the Arihants (souls who have conquered inner enemies like anger and greed).
Namo Siddhanam: I bow in reverence to the Siddhas (fully liberated and perfect souls).
Namo Ayariyanam: I bow in reverence to the Acharyas (supreme leaders of the monastic order).
Namo Uvajjhayanam: I bow in reverence to the Upadhyayas (spiritual teachers and preceptors).
Namo Loye Savva Sahunam: I bow in reverence to all Sadhus and Sadhvis (monks and nuns) in the universe.
Eso Panch Namoyaro: This five-fold salutation.
Savva Pavappanasano: Destroys all sins and negative karma.
Mangalanam Cha Savvesim: And amongst all auspicious and blessed things.
Padhamam Havai Mangalam: Is the first and foremost auspicious one.
